## Brand & Style

This design system blends ultra-luxury hospitality management with rigorous operational logistics. The visual tone is grounded, commanding, and serene—evoking high-end estate management, boutique private islands, and sommelier-level precision. It rejects sterile enterprise dashboard tropes in favor of warm tactile linen backgrounds, deep evergreen structure, and warm polished brass accents.

### Visual Style
- **Warm Editorial Minimalist / Quiet Luxury**: The UI draws heavy inspiration from classic architectural estate journals, warm bespoke stationery, and fine horology.
- **Atmospheric Contrast**: Grounded by an authoritative dark deep-forest sidebar (`#081C15`) juxtaposed against crisp parchment cards (`#FFFFFF`) on warm linen ground (`#FAF9F5`).
- **Precision Detailing**: Thin hairline champagne rules, micro uppercase tracking on technical badges, and editorial serif headline anchors elevate mundane logistical operations (dock bays, temperature monitors, gate registers) into an art of hospitality governance.

## Colors

The color palette is derived from natural organic resort textures: deep Indonesian rainforest foliage, burnished antique brass fittings, unbleached linen fabric, and warm champagne metallics.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#0D2818` / Forest)**: Communicates supreme authority and architectural permanence. Used for dominant navigation backdrops, key action CTAs, and highest-level typographic metrics.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` / Brass)**: Luxury accent role. Emphasizes active navigation indicators, key icons, badges, attention rings, and micro status pulses.
- **Tertiary (`#43654E` / Sage)**: Positive system health, compliant HACCP temperatures, and verified food safety statuses.
- **Neutral (`#FAF9F5` / Linen)**: Serves as the overarching canvas surface, softening eye fatigue compared to harsh sterile blue-whites.
- **Warning Accent (`#C87D28` / Saffron)**: High-visibility urgency role for short-dated perishables, reefer dock inspections, and countdown timers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs a high-contrast literary serif display family (**Playfair Display**) with an exceptionally clean, geometric humanist grotesque (**Plus Jakarta Sans**).

### Rules for Application
- **Serif for Editorial Context**: All primary page headers, key numerical telemetry totals (`title-metric`), and modal titles must be set in Playfair Display. This immediately signals curatorial prestige rather than generic warehouse inventory software.
- **Sans for Functional Legibility**: All data points, purveyor names, driver tags, status states, and timestamps are rendered in Plus Jakarta Sans.
- **Technical All-Caps Labels**: System subheaders, section flags, and operational state headers use `label-caps` (uppercase, 10.5px, 0.12em to 0.18em tracking) in brass or muted forest tones to impart structured military/estate discipline.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive fixed-rail and flexible canvas paradigm:
- **Left Navigation Rail**: Fixed width of `264px` (`sidebar-w`) spanning full viewport height with independent scroll and high-contrast styling.
- **Main Viewport Canvas**: Padded dynamically by the rail width, with maximum centered content constraints at `1380px` (`content-max-w`).
- **Grid Structure**: 12-column dynamic flex/grid setup. Metric cards form 4 columns (`col-span-3`), split operational streams use a 7:5 asymmetric ratio (`col-span-7` for live arrival queues, `col-span-5` for environmental cold storage metrics).
- **Rhythm**: Standard baseline spacing steps at `0.5rem` (8px), `0.75rem` (12px), `1rem` (16px), and `1.5rem` (24px). Section gaps maintain a generous `2rem` (32px) to preserve estate breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system is achieved via delicate tonal nesting and warm, tinted micro-shadows rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiering**:
  - Base canvas: Warm unbleached linen (`#FAF9F5`).
  - Container modules: Solid pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with thin champagne borders (`#E8E3D7`).
  - Recessed card compartments & action strips: Tonal linen tints (`#F5F2EB`).
- **Tonal Shadows**:
  - Cards: `0 2px 14px rgba(20, 54, 40, 0.03)` to `0 4px 24px rgba(20, 54, 40, 0.05)`. Green-tinted ambient occlusion ensures shadows look organic and soft rather than muddy gray.
  - Sidebar: Deep elevation drop `2px 0 30px rgba(8, 28, 21, 0.35)` to clearly separate navigation from ambient data flow.
- **Frosted Glassmorphism**:
  - Fixed top bar uses `bg-linen-50/90` with `backdrop-blur-xl` and a hairline `#E8E3D7` bottom border.
- **Accent Strip Anchors**: Primary queue cards utilize a left-edge `w-1` vertical solid status pillar (e.g., Brass for pending verification, Forest for staging, Sage for security clearance).

## Shapes

The design uses a restrained, tailored corner aesthetic with roundedness level 1 (`Soft`):
- **Base Cards & Modules**: `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px) provides an approachable yet structured estate ledger format.
- **Input Fields & Pill Actions**: Full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px) is reserved exclusively for interactive quick-action buttons, global search bars, header pills, and status tags.
- **Avatar & Icon Badges**: Circular (`rounded-full`) with delicate 1px brass or champagne framing.
- **Inner Nested Panels**: `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px) ensures nested child elements harmoniously fit inside parent containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Luxury Action)**: Background `forest-900` or `forest-800`, text `linen-50`, border `brass-500/40`, rounded-full. Hover shifts to `forest-700` with subtle upward lift (`-translate-y-0.5`).
- **Secondary (Tertiary Utility)**: Background `linen-100`, border `champagne`, text `forest-900`, rounded-full. Hover shifts to `white` with border tinting `brass-400/50`.
- **Icon Action Buttons**: 36px to 40px rounded-full containers, border `champagne`, background `linen-100`, housing centered Material Symbols.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Pill Shape**: Padding `py-0.5 px-2.5`, text size `11px`, font weight `600`.
- **Sage (Optimal / Verified)**: `bg-sage-50 text-sage-700 border border-sage-500/30`.
- **Saffron (Urgent / Warning)**: `bg-saffron-50 text-saffron-700 border border-saffron-500/30`.
- **Brass (Active Phase / Staging)**: `bg-brass-100 text-brass-800 border border-brass-300`.

### Cards & Telemetry Blocks
- **Structure**: Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), rounded-xl (`12px`), bordered with hairline champagne (`#E8E3D7`).
- **Interior Layout**: Header label in `label-caps`, center numerical stat in `title-metric` serif, and a docked bottom metadata footer in `bg-linen-50/80` with a top champagne separator line.

### Input Fields
- **Search & Filter Bars**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `bg-linen-100`, border `champagne`, leading 18px icon in brass tone, text in `forest-900`. Active state shifts background to white with a soft brass outline ring (`ring-1 ring-brass-500/30`).

### Interactive Lists & Rows
- **List Items**: Light linen ground (`#FAF9F5`), rounded-lg, bordered with `champagne/70`. On hover, transitions to `linen-100/60` and borders glow softly with `brass-400/40`.
- **Leading Indicator**: Monogrammed circle counter in `forest-900` or `linen-200` with high-contrast serif numbering.
